Hi, I'm Minh. People who know me would describe me as persevering, sharp, thoughtful and supportive: I keep going long after a first attempt fails, and I am always there when someone needs a hand. My passion for chemistry began in eighth grade, when “Science Tornado”, my school's science fair, sparked a curiosity that has only grown since.

What thrills me most is turning chemical principles into something people can actually use. Building a chemistry-based application that genuinely helps people in their daily lives makes me feel like a true inventor. Looking ahead, I want to develop clean, sustainable materials for every corner of modern life, materials that are cheaper to source while making the products built from them even more useful.

Beyond chemistry, I hold a 1700 Rapid rating on Chess.com, I am a die-hard Manchester City fan, and I once lost 8 kg over a three-month summer break by jogging every single day. I can happily spend hours at the gym, playing football with friends, playing chess online, practicing the piano, cooking up something new in the kitchen, or, I'll admit, scrolling through friends' social media a little longer than I should.
